About

I'm an Associate Security Analyst at Tenax, where I get experience on both the offensive and defensive sides of cybersecurity. My work includes penetration testing, external assessments, and social enginerring, while also spending time in the SOC investigating alerts, analyzing logs, and responding to potential threats. My defensive work involves conducting security investigations by correlating events, reviewing evidence, tracing suspicious activity, identifying potential indicators of compromise, and determining the root cause and impact of security incidents.

Before Tenax, I worked the service desk at Solutions Management Group, handling Active Directory access and escalating suspected account compromises to the security team. I have associate degrees in Cybersecurity and Digital Forensic Investigation from DMACC, and I serve as Secretary of ISSA Des Moines.

I'm especially passionate about red teaming and digital forensics. I enjoy the challenge of thinking like an attacker, finding vulnerabilities, and digging into the details of an investigation to understand what happened.
